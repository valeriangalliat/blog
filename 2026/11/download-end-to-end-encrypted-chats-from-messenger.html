Download end-to-end encrypted chats from Messenger

November 22, 2026

I like to periodically archive my data from online platforms, just in case something happens and they lose whatever I had on there (in the case of Messenger, my chat history).

This shit definitely happens in the wild, like that time Google Drive lost months of data for some users. 💀

Facebook’s plaintext chats are already part of Meta’s own Export your information tool, and that one seems OK.

However end-to-end encrypted chats need to be exported separately, from Messenger in Privacy & safety > End-to-end encrypted chats > Message storage > Download message storage data.

But in my experience (and not just mine apparently) those exports are half broken, missing random conversations and attachments. I could not get it to do a proper export that actually contains all my data.

So I made a Messenger Archive, a browser extension that reuses the same underlying mechanism as Messenger’s own export tool, but that actually works.

Not sure if it’s gonna keep working in the future if Facebook updates whatever functions the extension relies on, but as of today it allowed me to export all my chats and their attachments. Sharing in case it can be useful to anyone else.
